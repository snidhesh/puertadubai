Career Journey and Background

My real estate journey began after earning dual Bachelor’s Degrees in Business Marketing and Business Management from San Jose State University in 2003. I bought my first home at 22 years old and quickly developed a passion for real estate investing, flipping properties, and identifying opportunities with long-term value.

Early on, I worked alongside my father at his car dealership in Las Vegas, where I helped manage the company’s marketing, financing, and daily operations. But when financial challenges forced my father to shut his businesses down, my entire life changed. I was faced with a choice: either make it in real estate or move back home with my mom and apply for a corporate job.

I chose to bet on myself.

At the time, I had no financial safety net,
no wealthy background, and no outside
investors funding my journey.
My mother loaned me
$10,000 to help cover
my bills while I pursued real
estate full time, and those first three
years were the hardest years of my life
financially, emotionally, and mentally.
There were moments I didn’t know
how I would make it through.

What ultimately changed my life was learning how to truly connect with God. Through faith, persistence, and relentless work ethic, I kept going even during the seasons where nothing seemed to be working. Real estate was never a side job for me — I went all in from day one and stayed committed no matter how difficult things became.

Fast forward years later, I was blessed to build a successful luxury real estate business, invest in properties and businesses, and eventually help buy a home for my father alongside my business partner. Since then, I’ve continued supporting my family and giving back whenever possible.

That journey is also why philanthropy and helping others are so important to me. I know what it feels like to have nothing. I know what it feels like to need people to help you purely from the goodness of their hearts. Along my journey, there were angels who believed in me during the hardest chapters of my life, and I will never forget that.

Today, I am proud to say I am completely self-made. I founded Mahsheed Luxury Real Estate in 2006 to provide a higher level of service, integrity, and connection within the luxury real estate space. Over the years, we’ve represented professional athletes, entrepreneurs, executives, and families searching for a lifestyle where they can truly thrive.

With a combination of competence, commitment, compassion, and nearly two decades of full-time experience in luxury real estate, my goal has always been to make clients feel supported, protected, and genuinely cared for throughout every transaction.

As someone who speaks both fluent Farsi and English, I’m also able to communicate with clients from diverse backgrounds with ease and clarity.

Real estate changed my life — and I’m incredibly grateful every day that I never gave up on my dream.
